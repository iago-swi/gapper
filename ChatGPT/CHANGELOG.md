# Neon Circuit changelog

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-21

### Added

- **Quit** on the game-over screen returns you to the start screen with your score saved.
- The title screen shows the version number beside “1986 ARCADE REMIX”.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-20

### Added

- **This device** keeps your ten best scores with initials, level and date. Your previous personal best carries over.
- **Everyone** shows the ten best Neon Circuit scores across players and gives your rank when you finish outside the top ten.
- The game-over screen highlights your qualifying score and lets you sign it with up to three letters or digits.
- **High scores** on the start screen lets you browse both lists between runs.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-20

### Added

- **First person** lets you steer with just left and right. The ship sets off automatically and follows corners on its own.
- Your steering choice is remembered the next time you play.
- A hidden practice mode lets you fly through the seeker. Practice runs are excluded from your personal best.

### Changed

- In **Third person**, pressing the opposite direction reverses the ship immediately, even halfway along a rail.
- A sideways turn still works up to 30% of a rail past the junction. The unfinished rail earns no points.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-19

### Added

- Trace every rail while a pink seeker chases you across three repeating layouts. Each new rail earns 10 points.
- Cut a temporary gap behind your ship to force the seeker to take another route.
- Steer with the arrow keys, WASD, a swipe or the on-screen direction pad.
- Play in the browser or download the standalone game for offline play.
