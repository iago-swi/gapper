How to play

Light every violet rail to clear the circuit. Each new rail adds 10 points.

Steer
Use the arrow keys or WASD. Your ship keeps moving. Queue a turn just before a junction.
Cut a gap
Press Space to break the last rail you crossed. Both ships must go around until it repairs. One gap can be open at a time.
Evade
The pink seeker hunts you. A collision costs one of your three lives. Lit rails stay lit.
Pause
Press P or use the cabinet button. On touchscreens, use the direction pad and Cut gap.
