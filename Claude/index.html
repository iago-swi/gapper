Lightloom

Run your shuttle along every thread. Close a square and it lights up. Moths hunt the light, but a freshly lit square stuns the ones close by.

Point the way with the arrow keys or W A S D. On a touch screen, swipe. The shuttle keeps going. ← and → (or A D) turn it left or right. On a touch screen, tap that side. P pauses, M mutes, F fills the screen.
